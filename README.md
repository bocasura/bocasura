# Bocasura

Bocasura is a private online companionship club for adults. It connects men, called Patrons, with women, called Muses, for one-to-one conversation through messages and calls. All introductions and companionship take place online.

Our public projects explore the practical side of staying close across distance. Find a shared hour for a call, draw a conversation card, or read the studies behind how people connect online.
